Digital Protection Solutions —
What's available and what it actually does

A practical comparison of every layer of protection we can put in place. Free built-in tools, whole-home network controls, and third-party monitoring apps — and where each one falls short on its own.

Last updated: April 2026 — pricing and platform behavior verified
Tier 1

Free Built-In Controls

Every major platform ships with parental controls. They're free, reasonably effective, and already on your devices. The gap: they have almost no visibility — you can set rules but you won't know what was attempted, bypassed, or slipping through.

Apple Screen Time Google Family Link Amazon Kids Console Controls
Key limitation: No alerts, no reports, no visibility. You set it and hope it's working.
Tier 2

Whole-Home Network Controls

Router and network-level filtering covers every device on your WiFi — phones, tablets, game consoles, smart TVs, even friends' devices when they visit. Powerful inside the home. The gap: the moment a device switches to cellular data, it bypasses everything.

Eero Plus Circle Gryphon CleanBrowsing
Key limitation: Home WiFi only. Cellular data, school networks, and friends' WiFi are completely unprotected.
Tier 3

Third-Party Monitoring Apps

Device-installed apps that follow your child everywhere — home WiFi, school network, cellular data, anywhere. Provide real visibility: activity reports, real-time alerts, and in some cases AI-powered monitoring of texts, emails, and social media for concerning content.

Bark Qustodio Canopy Aura Life360
Key limitation: Monthly subscription required. Must be installed on each device individually. iOS/iPad caveat: Apple restricts background monitoring — no third-party app can continuously scan device content on iOS. Apps handle this differently; see the iOS Reality Check below.

Tier 3 — Third-Party Monitoring Apps
iOS / iPad Reality Check — this affects every app below

Apple's privacy policies prevent any third-party app from continuously monitoring device content in the background on iPhone or iPad. This is an Apple platform restriction — not a flaw in any specific app. The practical effect: on iOS, deep message and social media scanning requires a sync event (home WiFi, desktop app, or a sync cable). Web filtering and app blocking, however, can run continuously via a VPN/device profile and travel with the device everywhere. The apps below handle this differently — Bark relies entirely on sync; Qustodio and Canopy use a device profile for always-on content filtering.

Best for Android / Older Teens
Bark
$14/mo · $99/yr (Premium)
$49/yr Bark Jr · Unlimited devices
AI-powered monitoring that reads texts, emails, and 30+ social media platforms for signs of bullying, depression, predators, and explicit content. Alerts only when something concerning is found. Best on Android — on iOS/iPad, device-level monitoring requires a home sync, but cloud accounts (Gmail, iCloud email, Google Drive, YouTube) are monitored anywhere since they use OAuth APIs, not device WiFi. WhatsApp, iMessage, Snapchat, and TikTok DMs are end-to-end encrypted — no app on iOS can read those.
  • AI monitoring of texts, email, 30+ social platforms
  • Cloud accounts (Gmail, iCloud email, Google Drive, YouTube) monitored anywhere — no WiFi needed
  • Android: fully live monitoring on cellular everywhere
  • Location tracking, screen time & web filtering
  • iOS/iPad device content: retroactive scan at home sync only
  • iOS: TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, Discord — zero coverage (Apple blocks all apps)
iOS families note: Bark's value on iPad is real — but narrow. Cloud account monitoring works everywhere. Device content monitoring is retroactive (catches it when they get home). Bark's own solution is the Bark Phone (Android hardware they fully control). For iPad content protection away from home, pair with Qustodio or Gryphon HomeBound.

Recommended Combinations by Situation

VPN conflict — pick one per device: HomeBound, Qustodio, Canopy, and Bark's iOS web filter all run as a VPN on iPhone/iPad. iOS only allows one active VPN at a time. The combos below are designed around this — never pair HomeBound with any app-based VPN solution.

By Age

Ages 5–9

🧒 Young kids — iPad

Apple Screen Time Gryphon AX Canopy

Gryphon covers the whole home — consoles, TVs, every WiFi device — and blocks ads. Canopy's AI visual filter travels with the iPad on cellular, catching explicit images even when URLs aren't blocked. Screen Time handles device rules and downtime. No monitoring needed at this age — focus is purely on blocking.

⚠️ Don't add HomeBound — Canopy's VPN already handles cellular coverage.
Ages 10–13 · iPad

📱 Tweens — iPad household

Apple Screen Time Gryphon AX Qustodio

Highest-risk age window — social media starts, online connections appear. Qustodio's device profile handles filtering everywhere on cellular, adds per-app time limits, and provides the best available visibility on iPad. Gryphon covers home network including consoles. Note: deep message/social scanning isn't possible on iOS — Qustodio sees browsing and app usage, not message content.

⚠️ Don't add HomeBound — Qustodio's VPN already handles cellular coverage.
Ages 10–13 · Android

📱 Tweens — Android household

Google Family Link Gryphon AX Bark

Android is where Bark earns its reputation — full live AI monitoring of texts, 30+ social platforms, and email everywhere, no sync required. Gryphon covers the home network. Family Link handles device-level rules. This is the strongest monitoring stack available at any age.

Ages 14–17

👦 Teens — monitoring over blocking

Gryphon AX Bark (Android) / Qustodio (iPad)

At this age the conversation shifts from hard blocking to awareness. Gryphon handles the home network and ad blocking. Bark (Android) or Qustodio (iPad) provides visibility — alerts when something concerning appears rather than locking everything down. Heavy-handed blocking tends to backfire with teens; monitoring with trust conversations is more effective.

By Situation

🎮 Gaming-heavy household

Console Native Controls Gryphon AX Qustodio

Console controls handle the Switch, PlayStation, Xbox natively. Gryphon catches everything the console controls miss at the network level — including smart TVs and streaming sticks that no app can touch. Qustodio covers phones and iPads everywhere.

🖼️ Explicit content is the #1 concern

Gryphon AX Canopy

Gryphon blocks at the network level — covers every device in the home. Canopy's AI visual scanner catches explicit images that slip past URL-based blocking, running as a device profile everywhere the iPad goes. The most complete content-blocking stack available on iOS. No HomeBound needed — Canopy already handles cellular.

🧓 Aging parent / senior safety

Hiya Robokiller Aura Incogni 2FA + Password Mgr

Hiya blocks scam calls before they ring. Aura monitors identity, credit, dark web, and sends fraud alerts. Incogni removes their personal info from data broker sites — cutting off scammers at the source before they can find a phone number or address. 2FA on email and banking is the single highest-impact account protection step.

🏠 Full household — kids and an aging parent

Gryphon AX Qustodio (kids' iPads) Bark (Android kids) Aura (senior) Hiya (senior phone)

Gryphon AX handles the whole home network for everyone — kids and parent alike — including consoles, smart TVs, and ad blocking. Qustodio or Bark covers kids' devices based on platform. Aura and Hiya protect the senior's identity and phone independently. Each layer is targeted; nothing overlaps or conflicts.

Supplemental Add-Ons

These aren't part of the parental control or identity theft decision — they layer on top of whatever stack you choose and address a specific gap.

Data Broker Removal

$10.75/mo · human-reviewed
