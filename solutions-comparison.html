Start with what’s built in

Apple Screen TimeIncluded with supported Apple devices

Billing: No separate subscription

Device fit: Compatible iPhone, iPad and Mac

Useful for: App and website activity reports, time limits, content restrictions and supported communication safeguards.

Know the limits: Not a parent-readable transcript of every conversation. Menus and features vary by OS. Screen Time has no general VPN-toggle lock.

Official product / pricing source · Official support / terms
Google Family LinkFree

Billing: No separate subscription

Device fit: Supported child Android and Chromebook devices

Useful for: App approvals, usage reports, supported time and content controls, selected notifications and location.

Know the limits: A parent iPhone app does not supervise a child iPhone. Supervision does not automatically stop at 13; current under-18 stop-supervision rules require parent approval. Location needs a connected, active device.

Official product / pricing source · Official support / terms
Microsoft Family SafetyCore controls are free

Billing: No mandatory Microsoft 365 subscription for core controls

Device fit: Windows, Xbox and supported Android features

Useful for: Activity reports, Windows/Xbox device limits and supported app/game limits. Web filtering uses Microsoft Edge.

Know the limits: Other browsers must be restricted for web enforcement. Parent iOS management is not child iOS monitoring. Do not assume location or drive-safety features.

Choose one optional paid tool

QustodioBasic $59.95/year · Complete $104.95/year

Billing: Annual billing; advertised card prices before optional promotion codes. Renewal not verified.

Device fit: Basic: 5 devices · Complete: unlimited family devices

Useful for: Multi-device rules and reports; supported message monitoring subject to plan and platform. iOS SMS/iMessage content and WhatsApp/LINE concerning-topic alerts use desktop-assisted sync.

Know the limits: iOS collection needs a compatible Mac/Windows computer, initial cable setup, running app and same Wi-Fi; automatic sync is hourly when available, not live away-from-home monitoring. Verify Complete entitlement. Optional Advance removal prevention supports iOS 15–26, not iOS 27+, in reviewed documentation.

Official product / pricing source · Official support / terms
Bark App$14/month or $99/year

Billing: Monthly or annual subscription. Optional Bark Sync: $6/month or $49/year; App Store subscriber route lists $79 one-time. Renewal not verified.

Device fit: Unlimited children and devices, with platform-specific features

Useful for: Supported content-risk alerts, web filtering, screen-time tools and location. iOS supported content scans are separate from filtering.

Know the limits: iOS device-content monitoring needs a supported scan method such as an existing compatible desktop or optional Bark Sync. Sync can scan on Wi-Fi away from home; alerts are delayed, not instantaneous. Not every social app or message is supported. Verify the current monitoring matrix.

Official product / pricing source · Official support / terms
Canopy$8.33 / $9.99 / $12.50 monthly equivalents

Billing: Billed annually. Rounded advertised equivalents, not monthly payments or verified annual invoice totals. Renewal not verified.

Device fit: Essential: 1 · Plus: up to 3 · Premium: up to 10

Useful for: Supported explicit web-content filtering and separate saved-photo sexting alerts; capabilities vary by OS and permissions.

Know the limits: Not universal in-app message monitoring. Private/encrypted folders are excluded from saved-photo scans. iOS Removal Prevention requires separate setup; default alerts do not prevent removal. Do not equate VPN-attributed traffic with additional data use.

Review the router you already own

eero Plus$9.99/month or $99.99/year

Billing: Auto-renewing subscription; compatible hardware separate

Device fit: Compatible eero home network

Useful for: Network content filters, scheduling and activity history, with bundled services. Free eero profiles and pause controls may already be enough.

Know the limits: Home-network filters do not automatically follow devices onto cellular. Bundled privacy VPN is not equivalent to off-network parental filtering.

Official product / pricing source
TP-Link HomeShieldParental controls starting at $2.99/month or $17.99/year

Billing: Model/account/region dependent; separate Security+ and bundle prices are not required parental-control fees

Device fit: Compatible TP-Link router or Deco hardware

Useful for: Free basic controls plus optional advanced parental controls on compatible hardware.

Know the limits: Confirm features and renewal in the actual app/account. Router controls cover connected traffic, not every app or cellular connection.

Optional tools for adult needs

AuraFamily: observed $20/month equivalent annual promotion; other official copy $32

Billing: Conflicting seasonal offers; exact annual charge, renewal and feature inclusions require checkout verification

Device fit: Adults/devices depend on the selected offer

Useful for: Identity and financial alerts, device-security tools and consent-based Trusted Family Access.

Know the limits: Not a guarantee against scams. Family access must be granted and can be revoked. Insurance terms, eligibility and exclusions apply; identity coverage is not universal scam reimbursement. Separate cyber-insurance add-on is not assumed included.
