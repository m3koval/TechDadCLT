ResourcesA guide to your starting plan

Family online safety, one decision at a time.

Choose a practical baseline for children, independent adults or a household with both. No personal data is needed to use the plan finder.

Begin with the person, not the product

For children, use the family controls for their device: Apple Screen Time, Google Family Link or Microsoft Family Safety. For independent adults, begin with consent, account recovery, updates and a known-number callback routine—not child supervision.

Check the gap before adding a filter

Time limits, explicit web content and message-risk alerts are different needs. List the devices involved, check existing protections and test the scenario that concerns you. Multiple VPN-based filters can conflict. Do not treat more subscriptions as more protection.

Use the result as a starting point

The guide gives a native baseline, an optional tool only where relevant, and practical next steps. Exact OS and app compatibility must be checked before buying. It does not test installed settings, guarantee protection or replace an urgent call to your bank or account provider.

Assessment questions, answered

Do I need to buy an app?

No. Start with the controls included on your devices. A paid tool is only a candidate for a specific remaining need, not a requirement or a guarantee.

Does a router protect a phone away from home?

Home-network settings do not automatically follow a device onto cellular or another Wi-Fi network. Test supported device-level settings separately.

Can this guide read messages or check my devices?

No. This is a local decision guide, not a scan. Supported message-alert products have app, platform, permission and collection limits; alerts can be delayed.

Are my assessment answers sent to TechDad?

No. The plan finder keeps answers in page memory, not in a URL or browser storage. A downloaded summary stays on your device. Contacting TechDad is a separate choice and never copies your quiz answers.

What happens during the free onsite assessment?

In about 45 minutes, we discuss priorities and agreed devices and outline a written plan. Full setup and remediation are not included. Any implementation, subscriptions or hardware are quoted separately; a request is not a confirmed appointment.
