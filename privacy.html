Privacy & data use / November 25, 2026

A little information.
A clear purpose.

This notice describes how this website’s assessment request and optional analytics work.

Assessment requests

The form asks for your name, email, town or neighborhood, who you want help for and a general main concern. Phone is optional. Those details go directly from your browser to our existing Formspark form endpoint at submit-form.com so TechDad CLT / AMP IT Solutions can respond and discuss scheduling.

Formspark processes submissions under its own service and privacy terms. Submission acceptance does not prove email delivery to our inbox. Your browser displays an error if it cannot confirm acceptance; it keeps your typed answers on that page so you can decide whether to retry.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, financial records, identity documents, children’s names or sensitive incident details. We do not need them to discuss a visit. During agreed setup work, account owners enter their own credentials.

What stays in your browser

The plan finder runs locally and does not save or send your answers. The request form does not store a draft in browser storage. After a confirmed provider response, a short session marker lets the next page display an acknowledgment; it contains no form answers.

Your analytics preference is stored in local browser storage where available. This preference is specific to this browser and site. It can be changed using the controls in the footer.

Optional site analytics

This site retains its existing PostHog, Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights services to understand page use and performance, but loads them only after you allow analytics. They are disabled on the intake and acknowledgment pages and on pages opened with a query string or fragment. We do not intentionally send form answers, plan-finder answers, names, email addresses or phone numbers as analytics events.

PostHog automatic click/form capture, session recordings and automatic page capture are disabled. The site sends a basic page-view event only; URL parameters and referrers are excluded from that event. Analytics providers still process technical request information under their own policies. Hosting and the form provider may process IP addresses and request metadata as part of serving the website or processing submissions, independently of optional analytics.

Turning analytics off reloads this page without optional analytics. Previous events cannot be recalled by changing the browser preference. If your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, this site leaves optional analytics off.

Your choices and questions

You may call or email instead of using the form. For a question about a request, or to ask about access, correction or deletion, email hello@ampitsolutions.com. We do not publish an unverified fixed retention period or promise deletion from every provider system here; handling depends on the records and provider requirements involved.

Arranging help for another adult does not authorize access to their private accounts or information. We discuss permission directly before changes and sharing.
