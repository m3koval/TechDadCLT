Before we visit

A few good questions.

What’s included in the free assessment?

An approximately 45-minute onsite conversation and review of your priorities and agreed devices, with a written outline of next steps. It is not a comprehensive forensic audit, full device setup or a guarantee that every risk will be found.

What does setup cost?

Implementation is quoted at a fixed rate based on the agreed scope and devices. Optional software and hardware are itemized separately. You approve the quote before work begins; there is no obligation to proceed after the assessment.

Do we need a new router or a paid app?

Not necessarily. Built-in Apple, Google and Microsoft tools may cover your needs. We review existing equipment first and recommend a compatible paid tool only for a specific gap.

Can I arrange help for a parent?

Yes. Their choices and consent come first. Paying for a visit does not authorize access to another adult’s messages, accounts or transactions. We agree on what may be changed and shared with them directly.

Will you need my passwords?

You enter your own passwords and verification codes during agreed setup work. Do not send passwords, financial records or sensitive incident details through the request form or email.

Is this emergency scam support?

No. This request form is not an emergency channel or a 24/7 monitoring service. If money or account access is at risk, contact your bank or account provider using a known number immediately. Preserve evidence and see our scam-response guide.
