  • Start with the accounts

    Child accounts, family groups, recovery and a parent-only control passcode.

  • Make time and content settings intentional

    Schedules, app approvals, purchase restrictions and supported web controls.

  • Look inside the games and social apps

    Platform-native communication and privacy settings—not assumptions that a router can read every chat.

  • Test the boundaries

    For agreed implementation, check supported rules on home Wi-Fi and cellular, after a restart, and with the selected product’s removal settings.

Our default: less, but better chosen

Use what’s built in.
Add only what’s missing.

Apple Screen Time, Google Family Link and Microsoft Family Safety include useful reports and controls. Which one fits depends on the child’s device—not the phone a parent uses to manage it.

If you need supported message-risk alerts or more specific filtering, we consider one compatible paid product. Consumer VPN-based filters can conflict, so more apps do not automatically mean more coverage.
